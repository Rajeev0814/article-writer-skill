## What is the Article Writer Skill?

The Article Writer Skill is a structured, AI-powered framework built on the universal content crafting methodology. It breaks down the complex task of article creation into five manageable, sequential stages:

1. **Intake** — Capture your ideas
2. **Research** — Gather supporting information
3. **Generate** — Write and structure your content
4. **Validate** — Ensure quality and accuracy
5. **Review** — Polish and prepare for publishing

Unlike traditional writing tools that focus only on the writing phase, this skill encompasses the entire content creation lifecycle, ensuring consistency, quality, and platform-specific optimization.

## The 5-Stage Pipeline: Your Path to Quality Content

### Stage 1: Intake — Define Your Direction

**Purpose:** Establish the foundation for your article.

**What Happens:**
- Define your topic and main message
- Identify your target audience
- Set content guidelines and requirements
- Determine article length and format
- Establish tone and style parameters

**Questions to Answer:**
- What is the core topic?
- Who am I writing for?
- What problem does this article solve?
- What's the main takeaway?
- Are there specific keywords to target?

### Stage 2: Research — Build Your Knowledge Base

**Purpose:** Gather reliable, comprehensive information.

**What Happens:**
- Identify credible sources and references
- Collect data, statistics, and quotes
- Compile expert opinions and case studies
- Organize research materials
- Create an information hierarchy

**Research Methods:**
- Academic journals and papers
- Industry reports and whitepapers
- Expert interviews
- Case studies and real-world examples
- Existing articles and competitors' content

### Stage 3: Generate — Write and Structure

**Purpose:** Transform research into compelling prose.

**What Happens:**
- Create a detailed outline
- Write the article body with engaging narrative
- Add headers, subheaders, and formatting
- Include examples and illustrations
- Optimize for readability

**Key Elements:**
- Compelling headline
- Engaging introduction
- Clear body sections with subheadings
- Supporting examples and case studies
- Strong conclusion with call-to-action

### Stage 4: Validate — Ensure Quality and Accuracy

**Purpose:** Maintain high standards before publishing.

**What Happens:**
- Check factual accuracy
- Verify all citations and references
- Review grammar and style consistency
- Ensure compliance with platform guidelines
- Test links and media

**Validation Checklist:**
- Facts are accurate and current
- Sources are cited properly
- Grammar and spelling are correct
- Links are working
- Images are properly formatted
- Tone matches target audience

### Stage 5: Review — Polish and Prepare

**Purpose:** Final refinement before launch.

**What Happens:**
- Conduct editorial review
- Optimize for the target platform
- Add platform-specific metadata
- Format for publishing
- Schedule or publish

**Pre-Publishing Tasks:**
- Proofread one final time
- Add SEO metadata (title, description, tags)
- Optimize images and media
- Write compelling social media preview
- Plan distribution strategy

### Practical Workflow Example

Let's say you want to write a technical tutorial about Docker containers:

**Intake Phase:**
- Topic: "Docker for Beginners: A Complete Guide"
- Audience: Junior developers
- Length: 2,500 words
- Include: Code examples, visuals, practice exercises

**Research Phase:**
- Gather Docker documentation
- Collect best practices from experienced developers
- Find real-world use cases
- Compile beginner-friendly resources

**Generate Phase:**
- Outline: Introduction → Concepts → Installation → First Container → Advanced Topics → Conclusion
- Write with clear explanations and code examples
- Use headers to break up content
- Add screenshots and diagrams

**Validate Phase:**
- Check all code examples run correctly
- Verify Docker versions are current
- Test all links in the article
- Ensure beginner-friendly language

**Review Phase:**
- Final grammar and style check
- Optimize for Dev.to (code highlighting, formatting)
- Add tags: #docker #tutorial #beginners
- Write attractive meta description

## Common Challenges and Solutions

### Challenge 1: Writer's Block in the Generate Phase
**Solution:** Review your research materials and outline. Write bullet points first, then expand. The intake and research phases should have given you enough direction.

### Challenge 2: Content Feels Too Generic
**Solution:** Add personal experiences and unique perspectives during the generate phase. Include case studies and real examples.

### Challenge 3: Different Platform Audiences Don't Engage
**Solution:** Customize your article content for each platform's specific audience during the review phase. Research what performs well on each platform.

### Challenge 4: Validation Takes Too Long
**Solution:** Create a validation checklist in advance. Fact-check as you write, not after. Use tools like Grammarly for real-time suggestions.

### Challenge 5: Can't Keep Up With Publishing Schedule
**Solution:** Batch your content. Work through the pipeline for 4-5 articles at once, staggering the review and publishing phases.
